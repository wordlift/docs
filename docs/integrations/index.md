# Integrations

Integrations are connectors that embed WordLift in another product or move Knowledge Graph data between WordLift and the systems your team already runs. Find your CMS, store, automation or reporting tool below and open its guide.

Two other sections cover different needs. To operate WordLift directly (for example from an AI assistant or the command line), see [Interfaces and tools](/tools/). To connect WordLift in your own code, see [APIs and SDKs](/apis-and-sdks/).

## Content management and e-commerce

- **[WordPress plugin](/integrations/wordpress-plugin/):** Supports existing installs of the classic WordLift plugin, from installation to structured data output, widgets and customization. New customers start with [WordLift Cloud](/platform/knowledge-graph/schema-publishing/wordlift-cloud/) instead.
- **[WooCommerce](/integrations/woocommerce/):** WooCommerce SEO by WordLift adds structured data to your WooCommerce products and builds a Product Graph from the connections between products, brands, product categories and features.

## Automation

- **[Zapier](/integrations/zapier/):** Connects WordLift to thousands of apps through two Zapier actions, Ask Agent WordLift and Query Knowledge Graph (GraphQL), authenticated with your WordLift API key.
- **[Power Automate](/integrations/power-automate/):** Gives Power Automate, Power Apps and Logic Apps access to your Knowledge Graph through the GraphQL API. The connector is Premium in Power Automate and Power Apps, and Standard in Logic Apps.

## Reporting and SEO platforms

- **[Looker Studio](/integrations/looker-studio/):** Loads data from your Knowledge Graph into Looker Studio so you can blend it with Search Console data in your reports.
- **[BrightEdge](/integrations/brightedge/):** Explains how WordLift works alongside BrightEdge Autopilot for Schema, with WordLift as the source of truth for entities and relationships.

## Build your own

- **[APIs and SDKs](/apis-and-sdks/):** Use the WordLift APIs when no connector fits your stack and you want to build the connection yourself.
- **[Cloudflare integration](/apis-and-sdks/data-api/cloudflare-integration/):** Deploy a Cloudflare Worker that fetches WordLift JSON-LD from the Data API at the edge and inserts it into the initial HTML response.

## Other ways to connect

- **AI assistants:** Use the [MCP Server](/tools/mcp-server/) to work with your Knowledge Graph from an AI assistant.
- **Product feeds:** Use the [Product Knowledge Graph](/platform/knowledge-graph/product-knowledge-graph/) to build a graph from your product data.
- **Any website:** Load the WordLift Cloud script on any site with [Install WordLift Cloud](/platform/knowledge-graph/schema-publishing/wordlift-cloud/).

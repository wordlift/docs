# Plans and pricing

WordLift has two plans: Business+ and Enterprise. Enterprise includes everything in Business+, and Agent WordLift is included in both plans. Both plans start with a conversation with the WordLift team, and current prices are on the [WordLift pricing page](https://wordlift.io/pricing/).

## Business+

Business+ is for growing businesses that are ready to scale AI Search and SEO with guidance from WordLift's experts.

- **Every AI tool in the platform.** You get Agent WordLift, the [Knowledge Graph](/platform/knowledge-graph/), content creation and optimization, research and analysis, rank tracking and schema markup tools.
- **Dedicated strategic support.** A dedicated project manager runs strategy sessions, performance reviews and guided implementation with your team.
- **API and headless use.** Your Business+ key works with the WordLift APIs for headless and API-first setups, within the plan's usage limits.

Read the [Business+ plan page](/plans-and-pricing/business-plus/) for details, or [book a Business+ discovery session](https://wordlift.io/business-plus-get-started/) to start.

## Enterprise

Enterprise is for organizations that need a custom scope on top of everything in Business+. The scope can include custom Knowledge Graph and API integrations, higher API capacity, dedicated SLAs, SAML single sign-on, a custom domain for your Knowledge Graph, and dedicated onboarding and support. Pricing is a custom quote.

Read the [Enterprise onboarding guide](/plans-and-pricing/enterprise/) to see how Enterprise teams get started, or [send an Enterprise enquiry](https://wordlift.io/enterprise-enquiry/).

## Compare plans

The [Feature Matrix](/plans-and-pricing/feature-matrix/) lists each capability against Business+ and Enterprise, with a link to its documentation. Use it to check whether a capability is in your plan before you contact sales.

## Enterprise setup

Two features are available only on Enterprise. Each has its own setup page:

- **[SAML single sign-on](/plans-and-pricing/sso-saml/).** Your team signs in to WordLift through your identity provider.
- **[Custom domain](/plans-and-pricing/custom-domain/).** WordLift publishes your Knowledge Graph data under a domain you own, such as `data.example.org`.

## Developer access to the Resolve API

The Resolve API is the one self-serve exception: developers can get a key directly, without a sales conversation, and a free developer key includes 1,000 requests a month. See [Get access](/apis-and-sdks/#get-access) on the APIs and SDKs page.

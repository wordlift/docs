# AI visibility

AI visibility is the degree to which your brand and content are found, cited and recommended in answers generated by AI systems, such as AI assistants and Google's AI Overview and AI Mode.

## Why it matters

An AI-generated answer can satisfy a query without a click. Rankings, clicks and impressions then show only part of how people meet your brand. Being cited as a source, or recommended in the answer, is a separate form of visibility that classic SEO metrics do not capture.

AI visibility is one application of the WordLift [knowledge graph](/concepts/knowledge-graph/) platform, not the whole platform. The same graph also feeds structured data, internal linking and your own AI applications.

## How it works: what AI systems need from your site

WordLift's [AI Audit](https://wordlift.io/ai-audit/) checks how ready a site is for AI systems. It looks at these factors:

- **Machine readability.** Whether machines can extract what a page is and what it says.
- **Structured data quality.** Whether the page carries valid [schema.org](/concepts/schema-org/) markup that describes its entities.
- **JavaScript access.** Whether the content and markup are in the initial HTML, or appear only after scripts run.
- **AI agent accessibility.** Whether `robots.txt` and meta directives let AI agents reach the page.
- **Semantic context.** Whether the page makes its [entities](/concepts/entities/) and their relationships clear.

Structured data turns web content into machine-readable evidence, but it does not affect every AI system equally. It matters most when the system relies on Google, Bing, enterprise search or knowledge graph pipelines. Structured data alone does not get a site cited.

Where the markup lives also matters. Some AI crawlers and agentic tools read only the raw HTML a server returns, so they miss [JSON-LD](/concepts/json-ld/) that a script adds later. When AI visibility is a priority, prefer server-side delivery. Read [How it reaches your page](/concepts/json-ld/#how-it-reaches-your-page).

## How it is measured

The Agent WordLift [AI visibility workflow](/platform/agent-wordlift/workflows/ai-visibility-analysis/) measures two signals:

- **AI referral traffic.** Sessions that arrive from AI assistants, measured in your Google Analytics property, by source and landing page. This shows which pages AI assistants already send real visitors to.
- **Citations in AI Overview and AI Mode.** Whether your site is among the sources Google's AI Overview and AI Mode cite for the queries that matter to you. Visibility in one surface does not guarantee visibility in the other.

Two further measures explain the gaps:

- **Entity gaps.** An entity gap analysis compares the entities that cited sources cover with the entities on your page. It turns "not cited" into a list of entities and topics to add and mark up.
- **Query fan-out coverage.** AI search can expand one query into many related queries. The [Query Fan-Out API](/apis-and-sdks/api-reference/query-fan-out/create-ai-visibility-audit/) simulates this process for a URL or a text, then reports a coverage score for how well the content answers the generated queries.

## How WordLift supports it

- **Knowledge Graph and entities.** The [Knowledge Graph](/platform/knowledge-graph/) describes your entities and their relationships in one machine-readable source.
- **Server-side JSON-LD.** The [Data API](/apis-and-sdks/data-api/) delivers JSON-LD that you can place in the initial HTML of each page.
- **AI Visibility tools.** [Agent WordLift](/platform/agent-wordlift/), included in Business+ and Enterprise, has an AI Visibility tool family. It checks whether a query triggers an AI Overview, inspects the sources AI answers cite, queries AI Mode and analyzes AI Shopping results. Read [Tool families](/platform/agent-wordlift/tool-families/).

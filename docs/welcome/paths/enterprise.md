# Enterprise path

This path is for the people who run Enterprise onboarding: SEO leads, platform and data owners, and the IT and security teams they work with. It lists the pages to read in order and links out to each one. Different owners take different steps, so share each link with the person responsible.

## Discover

Frame the platform for the people evaluating fit and scale.

- **[Platform overview](/platform/).** See how Data Sources, the Knowledge Graph and Agent WordLift work together.
- **[Feature Matrix](/plans-and-pricing/feature-matrix/).** Compare what Business+ and Enterprise include. Enterprise includes everything in Business+.

## Onboard

Set up the workspace, then the identity and domain pieces your IT and admin teams own. Decide your dataset URI and custom domain before you populate the graph, because changing them later can require an identifier migration.

- **[Onboarding overview](/welcome/#onboarding-overview).** Follow the onboarding steps in order.
- **[Enterprise Onboarding Guide](/plans-and-pricing/enterprise/).** Plan the four phases (alignment, access validation, graph population and operations) and assign owners.
- **[Dashboard](/platform/dashboard/).** Sign in and find your Knowledge Graphs, tools and settings.
- **[SAML SSO](/plans-and-pricing/sso-saml/)** <span className="badge badge--primary">Enterprise</span>. Connect WordLift to your identity provider.
- **[Custom domain](/plans-and-pricing/custom-domain/)** <span className="badge badge--primary">Enterprise</span>. Publish your graph under a domain you control.
- **[Install WordLift Cloud](/welcome/install-wordlift-cloud/).** After the domain decision, add the launcher script that publishes your graph's structured data.

## Configure

Stand up the data and Knowledge Graph layers at the scale of your organization.

- **[Data Sources](/platform/data-sources/).** Connect the sources that feed your Knowledge Graph.
- **[Knowledge Graph](/platform/knowledge-graph/).** Learn how the graph stores your entities and relationships.
- **[Create a Knowledge Graph](/platform/knowledge-graph/create/).** Choose one primary ingestion path and validate it first.
- **[GraphSync](/platform/knowledge-graph/graph-sync/).** Build and maintain your content graph with GraphSync, the recommended path for Enterprise.

## Activate

Run Agent WordLift on the same core SEO and content jobs, across a large estate. Agent WordLift is included in Enterprise.

- **[Agent WordLift](/platform/agent-wordlift/).** See what Agent WordLift does and how to reach it.
- **[Workflows](/platform/agent-wordlift/workflows/).** Pick a ready-made workflow for a specific SEO or content task.
- **[Content Generation](/platform/agent-wordlift/content-generation/).** Generate content from the data in your Knowledge Graph.

## Expand

Extend the platform programmatically once it is part of your stack.

- **[APIs and SDKs](/apis-and-sdks/).** Connect your graph to your own systems and applications.
